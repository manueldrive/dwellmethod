Guided Dwell

Pick a passage and work through each corner with the questions below. Then fill in the center: the truth it shows you about God, and what that means for you.

Pre-study prayer

Lord, grant me the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of who You are. Correct any lies I believe. Let my study increase my love for You and others, not my pride. Amen.

Perspective

Step out of your modern point of view.

  • Who wrote this book?
  • Who was it written to?
  • When was it written?
  • Why was it written?

Words

Put off assumptions and find the intended meaning.

  • What words need clarity? Compare other translations for help.
  • What words are repeated or emphasized? Why do you think that is?

Connections

See how the Bible is one unified story.

  • Read the surrounding chapters. How does this fit the whole book?
  • Follow cross-references. Where else does this theme show up?

Questions

Lean in. Don’t be afraid to be curious.

  • What questions came up as you read? Who in your community could you ask?
  • Did anything confuse or bother you? Why?
The central truth
GOD IS…

What does this passage teach about who God is and how He works?

THEREFORE, I…

What does this reveal about who He made me to be?

Process

Do you believe the central truth from the passage? Why or why not?

What lies make it hard for a person to believe this truth?

How will this truth shape what you think, say, and do?

Dwell

Building blocks for Biblical literacy.

True transformation comes from the Holy Spirit. Dwell is a rhythm to slow down across five readings so Scripture can dwell in you richly.

Begin with Discover
D Discover

Get the lay of the land.

  • Use a study Bible or trusted resource to set the scene:
    Author • Audience • Time • Purpose
  • Now put the resource away. Read slowly and note anything that stands out or raises a question for you.
Advance to Word Work
W Word Work

Inspect the text up close.

  • Highlight repeated words and anything confusing.
  • Look up definitions or compare translations for those words.
  • Sum up the passage in one sentence.
Advance to Expand
E Expand

Step back for the bigger picture.

  • Read the surrounding chapters to see how this fits the whole book.
  • Follow cross-references to see where else this idea appears.
Advance to Learn
L Learn

Uncover the timeless truth.

  • What was the takeaway for the first people who read this?
  • What does this teach us about who God is and how He works?
  • Check your findings in community, notes, or commentaries.
Advance to Live
L Live

Put the truth into practice.

  • What cultural or personal lies does this truth expose?
  • How will this shape your prayers, thoughts, words, or actions today?

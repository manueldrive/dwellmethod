Dwell
Building Blocks for Biblical Literacy

Slow down. Re-read.
Be transformed.

True transformation comes from the Holy Spirit. Dwell is simply a rhythm to slow down across five readings so Scripture can dwell in you richly. Like the tree planted by streams in Psalm 1, it helps you delight, take root, and listen. Practice one step or stack all five.

Begin with Discover
D Discover
Step One • Discover

Find the historical background.

  • Use a study Bible or trusted resource to set the scene:
    Author • Audience • Time • Purpose
  • Set aside outside resources to let the passage speak for itself. Read slowly and record what stands out to you.
Advance to Word Work
W Word Work
Step Two • Word Work

Inspect the text up close.

  • Underline repeated words, lists, comparisons, and key connecting words.
  • Look up simple definitions for any words that seem unfamiliar or confusing.
  • Write out the main point of the passage in one clear sentence.
Advance to Expand
E Expand
Step Three • Expand

Connect to the bigger picture.

  • Read the surrounding chapters to see how this passage fits into the whole book.
  • Compare two different translations to clear up tricky phrasing.
  • Follow cross-references in the margins to see where else this idea appears throughout Scripture.
Advance to Learn
L Learn
Step Four • Learn

Uncover the timeless truth.

  • What was the main takeaway for the very first people who read this?
  • What does this passage teach us about who God is and how He works?
  • Check your findings in community, study notes, or commentaries to verify what you learned.
Advance to Live
L Live
Step Five • Live

Put the truth into practice.

  • What cultural or personal lies does this truth expose?
  • How will this truth shape your prayers, thoughts, words, or actions today?

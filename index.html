Dwell
Building Blocks for Biblical Literacy

Slow down. Re-read. Be transformed.

Good study tools help you slow down; the Holy Spirit brings true transformation. Dwell helps you build the habits to keep you focused across five readings of a passage—quieting distractions so you can truly hear God speak.

Begin with Discover
D Discover
Step One • Discover

Find the historical background.

  • Use the book introduction in a study Bible to find your place:
    • Author: Who wrote this?
    • Audience: Who received it?
    • Time: When was it written?
    • Purpose: Why was it written?
  • Read straight through the passage once just to map the scene and notice the characters.
Advance to Word Work
W Word Work
Step Two • Word Work

Inspect the text up close.

  • Underline repeated words, lists, comparisons, and key connecting words.
  • Look up simple definitions for any words that seem unfamiliar or confusing.
  • Write out the main point of the passage in one clear sentence.
Advance to Expand
E Expand
Step Three • Expand

Connect to the bigger picture.

  • Read the surrounding chapters to see how this passage fits into the whole book.
  • Compare two different translations to clear up tricky sentences.
  • Follow 1–2 related verses in the margins to see where else this idea appears.
Advance to Learn
L Learn
Step Four • Learn

Discover the timeless truth.

  • What was the main takeaway for the very first people who read this?
  • What does this passage teach us about who God is and how He works?
  • Now check commentaries, study notes, or talk with others to verify what you learned.
Advance to Live
L Live
Step Five • Live

Put the truth into practice.

  • What everyday thoughts or habits does this truth challenge?
  • What is one concrete way this truth will change your words and actions today?

Dwell

Building blocks for Biblical literacy.

Dwell is a Bible study rhythm to help you slow down across five readings. True transformation comes from the Holy Spirit; this guide’s only goal is to help you stay focused by giving fresh intention each time you read. Practice one step individually or stack all five.

Get the lay of the land.

  • Use a study Bible or trusted resource to set the scene:
    Author • Audience • Time • Purpose
  • Now put the resource away. Read slowly and note anything that stands out or raises a question for you.

Inspect the text up close.

  • Highlight words that repeat, feel unfamiliar, or leave you confused.
  • Look up definitions or compare translations to gain clarity around them.

Step back for the bigger picture.

  • Read the surrounding chapters to see how this fits the whole book.
  • Follow cross-references to see where similar themes appear elsewhere in Scripture.

Uncover the timeless truth.

  • What was the takeaway for the first people who read this?
  • What does this teach us about who God is and how He works? Check your findings in community, notes, or commentaries.

Put the truth into practice.

  • What cultural or personal lies does this truth expose?
  • How will this shape your prayers, thoughts, words, or actions today?

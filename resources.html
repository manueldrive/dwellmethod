Tools

The questions in each corner stay the same. The tools you use to answer them grow with you. Start at Level 1, and move inward when you’re ready.

Level 1 · StartLevel 2 · GrowLevel 3 · Go deeper

Perspective

THE QUESTIONWho wrote this, to whom, when, and why?

LEVEL 1 · START

The envelope: author, audience, time, and purpose

  • The book introduction in a study Bible
  • BibleProject book overview videos
LEVEL 2 · GROW

Genre and how to read it: story, poetry, letter, prophecy

  • BibleProject: How to Read the Bible
LEVEL 3 · GO DEEPER

Cultural background through commentaries

  • IVP Bible Background Commentary (Old and New Testament)

Words

THE QUESTIONWhat words repeat? What words need clarity?

LEVEL 1 · START

A dictionary and other translations

  • Bible Gateway: read a verse in several translations side by side
LEVEL 2 · GROW

Greek and Hebrew tools, or a concordance

  • Blue Letter Bible: tap a word to see the original and where else it appears
  • Bible Hub: interlinear and concordance
LEVEL 3 · GO DEEPER

Trace a word across the whole Bible

  • STEP Bible: follow one original word through Scripture
  • BibleProject word study videos

Connections

LEVEL 1 · START

The chapter before and after

LEVEL 2 · GROW

A cross-reference in another part of the Bible

  • OpenBible.info cross references
LEVEL 3 · GO DEEPER

Themes that run through all of Scripture

  • BibleProject theme videos

Guided SOAR

A simple way to read Scripture with God’s character at the center. Read it, look closer, see who He is, and live it out.

How it works

  1. ScriptureBefore you read, set the scene: who wrote it, when, why, and what comes before and after.
  2. ObservationWhile you read, define confusing words, explain hard ideas, and connect it to the rest of the Bible.
  3. God is… Therefore, I…What does this passage show about who God is? What does that mean for who He made you to be?
  4. ApplicationDo you believe it? What lies get in the way? How will you live it out this week?
  5. ReflectionPray it, write down your questions, and tell someone what God is teaching you.

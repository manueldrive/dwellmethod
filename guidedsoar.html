GOD IS…THEREFORE, I…
For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many. Mark 10:45
Scripture

Before reading, look up 2–3 of these:

  • Who wrote it
  • When it was written
  • Why it was written
  • What happens right before and after
Observation

Review 2–3 while reading to bring clarity:

  • Define confusing terms
  • Explain difficult concepts
  • Make connections to other parts of the Bible
Application
  • Do you believe the central truth from this passage?
  • What lies might keep a person from seeing this truth?
  • How can you live it out? (thoughts, words, actions)
Reflection
  • How can you ask God to help you live this out?
  • What questions do you still have about this passage?
  • Who can you bring your remaining questions to?
GOD IS…

What does this passage reveal about God’s character and purpose?

THEREFORE, I…

What does this reveal about who He made me to be?

Scripture

Mark 10:32–45

Author: John Mark

Date: Somewhere between 50–70 AD

Purpose: To show Jesus as the Son of God

Observation

Third time Jesus predicts His death.

It’s also mentioned multiple times in the OT. (Isaiah 53)

The disciples still did not get it and instead pursued earthly glory.

Glory – importance/honor

Application

I believe it, but like James and John, I still want people to notice me. The lie: I only matter if I’m in charge.

This week: stop keeping score, build my friends up, and do a chore nobody thanks me for.

Reflection

God, You gave Your life for me. Help me serve even when no one is watching.

Still wondering: what’s the “cup” (v.38), and why ask for glory right then? I’ll ask my small group leader.

GOD IS…

all powerful, yet He chooses to serve.

THEREFORE, I…

look not to be served but to serve.

How to think about each part of the template. Use it on your own, or to lead a group.

Pray

Lord, grant me the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of who You are. Correct any lies I believe. Let my study increase my love for You and others, not my pride. Amen.

Perspective

  • Who wrote this book? Who was it written to?
  • When was it written? Why?

New to this book?Read the intro in a study Bible, or watch the BibleProject Read Scripture video.

Words

  • What words or phrases repeat? What’s the point of the repetition?
  • What words need clarity? Look them up or compare translations.

Connections

  • Read the surrounding chapters. How does this fit the whole book?
  • Follow cross-references. Where else does this theme show up?
  • How would the first readers have understood this?

Questions

  • Read slowly, expecting to discover something.
  • Write down every question. Did anything confuse or bother you?
  • Circle the one you most want answered.
GOD IS…

What does this passage teach about who God is and how He works?

THEREFORE, I…

What does this reveal about who He made me to be?

Process

  • Do you believe the central truth from the passage?
  • What lies make it hard for a person to believe this truth?
  • How will this truth shape what you think, say, and do?
  • Bring any remaining questions to your community.

Leading a group?

  1. Do the study yourself first.
  2. Read the passage together slowly.
  3. Have everyone share their questions. Pick one together and discuss it.
  4. Work through the corners, then land on the central truth together.
  5. Ask: What is God inviting us to as a community?
